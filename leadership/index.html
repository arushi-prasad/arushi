How I approach design leadership

Over time, I’ve learned that building a design team is as much about creating the right conditions as it is about the work itself.
That means setting clear expectations, helping people grow, and building practices that make good decisions easier.
When people have the clarity and confidence to do their best work, the whole team moves forward.
