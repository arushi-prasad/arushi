Hover-responsive typography

01

Systems

Each letter measures its distance to the pointer and takes one of three weights. On a touch screen the nearest letter to a tap lights instead, so the behaviour is demonstrated rather than disabled. The label is on the paragraph, so a screen reader hears one word rather than seven letters.

Text reveal

02

Products, systems, and teams.

Three masked lines, each arriving from behind its own bottom edge with an 80 ms offset. The mask is the parent's overflow:hidden, so no clip path is needed and it stays crisp at any size. Under reduced motion the lines are simply already in place.

Colour transition

03

·

Five swatches crossing to the next hue in the active palette on a staggered delay. Auto is off by default and stops itself after one full cycle. A decorative loop that runs forever is a tax on every visitor who is not watching it.

Hover or focus it. Four properties move together; the link underneath still works.

The border takes the accent, the surface lifts one step, the card rises 3 px and the art field scales 6%. Four small changes read as one object responding. It is the same component class the Work page uses, not a copy of it.
