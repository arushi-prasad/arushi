Design Principles

A workshop on what design principles are, why a growing team needs them, and how to write a set that comes from inside the team rather than from a poster.

Workshop 01 · 7 September 2023

Agreeing what good means, before arguing about it

A senior designer can look at a screen and say it is wrong. That instinct does not scale past about a dozen people. This workshop was the first half of the fix: establishing what design principles are for, looking at how other teams write them, and then running the exercise that produces a set from inside the room.

What this is, and what it is not

My own deck, presented on 7 September 2023. It contains the discovery, not the conclusion: the argument for principles, the inspiration, and the exercise. It stops at “how might we create a set of our own”.

No approved set of principles came out of it that I can show. Rather than invent one, the page ends where the workshop ended. The slides on Spotify and Airbnb are those companies' own published principles, shown as inspiration.

Nine of the seventeen slides are shown. The rest are section transitions, two further inspiration examples, and one slide listing the four breakout teams by name, which is not published: individual personnel data does not appear anywhere on this site.
