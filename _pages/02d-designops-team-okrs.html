Design leadership / Strategy

Team OKRs

Design leadership is about creating the conditions for good work to happen: setting direction, building systems, improving experiences and helping teams grow. These OKRs reflect the priorities and outcomes I drove across the design organisation.

Building a high-performing design organisation

The team's OKRs were structured around five focus areas: design system implementation, critical journey transformation, design process optimisation, delivery excellence, and building a high-performing team. Together, these priorities established a clear direction for improving product experiences while strengthening the design organisation.
