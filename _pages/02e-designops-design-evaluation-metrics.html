Design Evaluation Metrics

Design Evaluation Metrics provided a structured way to assess the usability and overall effectiveness of the product experience.

The objective was to understand how well the experience aligned with user needs, expectations, and goals. The evaluation focused on five key dimensions: Discoverability, Learnability, Engagement, Efficiency, and Error Handling & Guidance.

Rather than treating the review as a fault-finding exercise, the intent was to identify usability gaps and opportunities for improvement across the experience.

A consistent 1–5 rating scale was used to evaluate each dimension, supported by specific criteria and practical examples. This helped create a measurable baseline for design decisions and future refinements.
