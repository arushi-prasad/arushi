Every workplace benefit, in one place.

WorkPerks is an employee benefits platform. It brings Flexi Pay, wellness, OPD, reimbursements, rewards and recognition into one employee experience, and it sits on top of the HR stack a company already runs rather than asking to replace it.

01 · Overview

Employee benefits are no longer insurance and meal cards. Companies are investing in a better employee experience, but the way benefits reach people hasn’t changed.

  • Then Insurance. Meal cards. Reimbursement.
  • Now Health, learning, experiences, family and financial wellbeing.
  • The gap More money is being spent. Employees still can’t find or use most of it.

The problem we are solving.

The money is already committed. What is missing is a single place where employees meet their benefits and HR can see the programme end to end.

The utilisation gap

Employees had access to benefits, but fragmented platforms and complex processes made them difficult to discover, understand and use.

05 · Design Principles

Five principles the product answers to.

The chapters before this one are a run of decisions. These are the five principles those decisions answer to: what the product connects, what it makes visible, what it makes clear, what it makes trustworthy, and who it is built for.

Each one is written as an instruction rather than as a value, so it can settle a question about a real screen.

  1. 01

    One ecosystem

    Bring benefits, rewards, claims and services together in one connected experience. Employees should not have to navigate multiple platforms to understand and use what their organisation offers.

  2. 02

    Make value visible

    Help employees understand their entitlements, available balances and the value they get from their benefits. Make financial and benefit information easy to discover and understand.

  3. 03

    Clarity before complexity

    Make eligibility, funding rules and next steps easy to understand before employees take action. Reduce ambiguity across benefit discovery, booking and redemption.

  4. 04

    Build trust into every interaction

    Make balances, transactions, claims and outcomes transparent and predictable. Every interaction should communicate progress and provide confidence in what happens next.

  5. 05

    Design for everyone in the ecosystem

    Create connected experiences for employees, HR teams and suppliers. A seamless employee experience depends on the workflows and interactions across the entire ecosystem.

06 · The Design System

One system, two very different products.

The employee app and the HR portal look like one product because they read the same tokens and the same components. Version 1.0 was documented in September 2026 as foundations, components, patterns and a token layer that ships as four files.

Every value starts from the WorkPerks marketing site, then is tightened for product use: a 4px grid, contrast that passes, a real error colour, and the states a marketing site never needs.

Flexi, the hard middle

The step where onboarding stops explaining and starts asking. Two screens carry it, the one that puts the decision and the one that takes the answer, and both were drawn to be refused as easily as accepted.

  • An employee has an annual allowance, a declaration window, a set of eligible heads and a consequence if they skip it.
  • They did not open a benefits app to do tax planning.
  • So the product explains the shape of the decision rather than the tax: the amount, the mechanic, and what happens if you do nothing.
  • Then it stops, because it is not qualified to give tax advice.
  • This was the most argued over surface in the product, and it is the one I would defend first.

Bringing product thinking, design, implementation and testing into one working loop.

I paired product judgement with AI assisted implementation to move from decisions to working software. The design gets run, found wanting and fixed in the same week it is drawn, rather than handed over and returned altered.

    Decide

  3. 03

    Generate

  5. 04

    Run

    On the device, at the route, in the same session.
